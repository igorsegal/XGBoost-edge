Active Learning: An EA That Only Asks for Human Confirmation on Its Most Uncertain Trades

MetaTrader 5 — Machine learning | 5 November 2026 at 13:17
512 0
Olamide Daniel Adebayo

Introduction

Every discretionary trader knows the setups that are easy to take, the ones that are easy to skip, and the handful in between that make you pause. This article builds a routing layer around that instinct. A small online classifier scores the next move, entropy turns the score into an uncertainty number, and the signal goes one of three ways: trade it automatically, discard it as noise, or put it in front of a human checkpoint. A human cannot click buttons inside the Strategy Tester, so the checkpoint is replaced there by mechanical proxies.

Building the router is the easy half. The harder half is finding out whether it does anything, and a backtest is easy to fool: pick a threshold on a run, report that same run, and compare against a weak benchmark. So the article follows a test protocol. The entropy ceiling is chosen on 2026.01.01 to 2026.06.30 by a rule fixed before any held-out run, then everything is judged on 2026.07.01 to 2026.09.14 against four controls: a trade-everything baseline, a matched-frequency control that thins the baseline at random to the same trade count, 50 seeded coin-flip runs, and an exact split of profit between auto-traded and queried trades.

The result is modest, and I would rather say so here. Routing loses far less than trading everything on all four symbol and timeframe legs, for example -9 647.95 for the baseline against 852.58 for confirm-all on GBPUSD M15. But on three of the four legs the routed runs' per-trade results sit inside the spread of random subsets of the baseline with the same trade count, so most of that improvement is simply trading less. The ceiling chosen in the tuning window lost money on every leg there. The pieces are worth building. The edge is not established.

Scope: this is a signal-routing and risk layer around a deliberately small classifier (price plus three indicators), not a production alpha model. All trading results come from Strategy Tester runs on MetaQuotes-Demo made on 2026.10.27, with a single data feed, zero commission and one eleven-week held-out window. The human decisions are modeled by proxies, so nothing here measures a human.

The idea comes from active learning: when labels are expensive, a model asks an annotator only about the cases it is least sure of. Replace "expensive label" with "expensive human attention" and it transfers to trading. Review everything and you start rubber-stamping; review nothing and you have surrendered control.


System Architecture

Three files split the work. ActiveConfirmLib.mqh holds the quantile estimator, the classifier, the entropy scorer and the shared enums and structs. ActiveConfirm_EA.mq5 owns the decision loop and everything that touches the trade server. ActiveConfirm_ProbView.mq5 replays the same math in a sub-window. The EA and the indicator include the same library, which carries an include guard, so they share the same formulas. The indicator's state can differ from the EA's, as the ProbView section explains.

Each new bar runs through one pipeline: build four features, score them, convert the score to uncertainty, and classify it against a fixed ceiling and a moving quantile. Fig. 1 shows where the thresholds sit on the entropy axis and Fig. 2 shows the decision path.

The Uncertainty Score

Uncertainty is the Shannon entropy of the classifier's predicted probability, in bits, so it runs from 0 to 1. A prediction of 0.5 scores 1.0 and a prediction near 0 or 1 scores near 0. Mathematically H(0) and H(1) are exactly 0; the code clamps p to 0.000001 to 0.999999 before taking logarithms, so the practical floor is about 0.00002 and the log is never evaluated at zero.

H(p) = −[ p · log₂(p) + (1−p) · log₂(1−p) ]

SafeSigmoid and Entropy

//+------------------------------------------------------------------+
//| SafeSigmoid - numerically stable logistic                        |
//+------------------------------------------------------------------+
double SafeSigmoid(const double z)
  {
   double zc=z;
   if(zc>35.0)
      zc=35.0;
   if(zc<-35.0)
      zc=-35.0;
   return(1.0/(1.0+MathExp(-zc)));
  }

//+------------------------------------------------------------------+
//| Entropy - normalized bit-entropy score                           |
//+------------------------------------------------------------------+
double Entropy(const double p)
  {
   double eps=0.000001;
   double pp=p;
   if(pp<eps)
      pp=eps;
   if(pp>1.0-eps)
      pp=1.0-eps;
   double h=-(pp*MathLog(pp)+(1.0-pp)*MathLog(1.0-pp))/MathLog(2.0);
   return(h);                    // ~0 near the ends (p is clamped), 1.0 at p=0.5
  }

SafeSigmoid clamps its input to plus or minus 35. That is not overflow protection, since MathExp(35) is nowhere near the limit of a double; it keeps the output a hair away from exactly 0 or 1, so the gradient and the clamped entropy stay well-behaved.

Entropy is a monotone function of the distance |p - 0.5|, so a ceiling on entropy is a ceiling on that distance. A ceiling of 0.99 skips probabilities between 0.441 and 0.559, 0.95 skips 0.369 to 0.631, 0.90 skips 0.316 to 0.684 and 0.85 skips 0.276 to 0.724. Routing on entropy and routing on |p - 0.5| therefore select the same signals, and the quantile tracker ranks them in the same order. Entropy gives a bounded scale that suits an input called a ceiling, nothing more, and this article claims no advantage of entropy over a plain probability threshold.


The Review Budget: An Online P² Quantile

The auto-skip ceiling is a fixed input. The boundary between auto-trade and ask-a-human cannot be fixed, because the entropy distribution can drift as market conditions change. What is fixed is the budget. InpReviewBudgetPct is the share of non-skipped signals that should reach the human checkpoint, and a P² streaming quantile (Jain and Chlamtac, 1985) tracks the threshold that delivers it. Skipped readings never reach the tracker, so 12 percent means 12 percent of the signals that survive the ceiling, not of all bars.

P² seeds its five markers from the first five observations and from then on nudges them with a parabolic fit, falling back to linear interpolation when the parabola would overshoot a neighbor. Buffering and sorting every value would grow without bound on a live EA, and P² avoids that.

//| CP2Quantile class declaration                                    |

class CP2Quantile

CP2Quantile::Update

//+------------------------------------------------------------------+
//| CP2Quantile: Update                                              |
//+------------------------------------------------------------------+
void CP2Quantile::Update(const double x)
  {
   //--- level 1: still filling the 5-observation seed buffer
   if(!m_ready)
     {
      m_init_buf[m_init_count]=x;
      m_init_count++;
      if(m_init_count<P2_MARKERS)
         return;
      //--- level 2: seed buffer full - sort it and set up all 5 markers
      ArraySort(m_init_buf);
      for(int i=0;i<P2_MARKERS;i++)
        {
         m_q[i]=m_init_buf[i];
         m_n[i]=i+1;
        }
      m_npos[0]=1.0;
      m_npos[1]=1.0+2.0*m_p;
      m_npos[2]=1.0+4.0*m_p;
      m_npos[3]=3.0+2.0*m_p;
      m_npos[4]=5.0;
      m_dn[0]=0.0;
      m_dn[1]=m_p/2.0;
      m_dn[2]=m_p;
      m_dn[3]=(1.0+m_p)/2.0;
      m_dn[4]=1.0;
      m_ready=true;
      return;
     }
   //--- level 1: locate the cell the new observation falls into
   int k=0;
   if(x<m_q[0])
     {
      m_q[0]=x;
      k=0;
     }
   else if(x>=m_q[4])
     {
      m_q[4]=x;
      k=3;
     }
   else
     {
      for(int i=0;i<4;i++)
        {
         if(m_q[i]<=x && x<m_q[i+1])
           {
            k=i;
            break;
           }
        }
     }
   //--- level 1: shift marker positions and desired positions
   for(int i=k+1;i<P2_MARKERS;i++)
      m_n[i]=m_n[i]+1;
   for(int i=0;i<P2_MARKERS;i++)
      m_npos[i]=m_npos[i]+m_dn[i];
   //--- level 1: adjust the three interior markers if they drifted too far
   for(int i=1;i<=3;i++)
     {
      double d=m_npos[i]-m_n[i];
      if((d>=1.0 && (m_n[i+1]-m_n[i])>1) || (d<=-1.0 && (m_n[i-1]-m_n[i])<-1))
        {
         int sd=(d>=1.0)?1:-1;
         double qnew=Parabolic(i,sd);
         //--- level 2: fall back to the linear formula if parabolic overshoots
         if(qnew>m_q[i-1] && qnew<m_q[i+1])
            m_q[i]=qnew;
         else
            m_q[i]=Linear(i,sd);
         m_n[i]=m_n[i]+sd;
        }
     }
  }

CP2Quantile::Parabolic, Linear, GetQuantile

//+------------------------------------------------------------------+
//| CP2Quantile: Parabolic                                           |
//+------------------------------------------------------------------+
double CP2Quantile::Parabolic(const int i,const int d)
  {
   double dd=(double)d;
   double term1=(m_n[i]-m_n[i-1]+dd)*(m_q[i+1]-m_q[i])/(m_n[i+1]-m_n[i]);
   double term2=(m_n[i+1]-m_n[i]-dd)*(m_q[i]-m_q[i-1])/(m_n[i]-m_n[i-1]);
   return(m_q[i]+(dd/(m_n[i+1]-m_n[i-1]))*(term1+term2));
  }

//+------------------------------------------------------------------+
//| CP2Quantile: Linear                                              |
//+------------------------------------------------------------------+
double CP2Quantile::Linear(const int i,const int d)
  {
   return(m_q[i]+d*(m_q[i+d]-m_q[i])/(m_n[i+d]-m_n[i]));
  }

//+------------------------------------------------------------------+
//| CP2Quantile: GetQuantile                                         |
//+------------------------------------------------------------------+
double CP2Quantile::GetQuantile(void) const
  {
   return(m_q[2]);                // middle marker tracks the target quantile
  }


The Classifier: Rolling Online Logistic Regression

The direction model is a logistic regression with four inputs, trained by batch gradient descent with an L2 penalty on the weights but not on the bias. It is warm-started: every InpRefitEveryBars bars, Fit() runs another InpGDIterations gradient steps on the current 300-bar window starting from the previous coefficients. Only Init() zeroes them, so the model keeps memory across refits.

p = σ(z), σ(z) = 1 / (1 + e^(-z)), z is β0 + β1·x1 + β2·x2 + β3·x3 + β4·x4

gradient step: β ← β − lr · ( (1/N)·Xᵀ(p−y) + λ·β )

Leaving the bias out of the penalty matters: penalizing it would pull the baseline prediction toward 50/50 whatever the class balance of the window.

COnlineLogReg::Fit and Predict

//+------------------------------------------------------------------+
//| COnlineLogReg: Fit                                               |
//+------------------------------------------------------------------+
bool COnlineLogReg::Fit(double &x_rows[][FEATURE_COUNT],double &y_rows[],
                         const int rows,const int iters,
                         const double lr,const double l2)
  {
   //--- level 1: refuse to fit on a window that is too thin to trust
   if(rows<m_min_train_rows)
     {
      m_ready=false;
      return(false);
     }
   double grad[FEATURE_COUNT+1];
   //--- level 1: batch gradient descent, L2-regularized on the weights only
   for(int it=0;it<iters;it++)
     {
      ArrayInitialize(grad,0.0);
      for(int r=0;r<rows;r++)
        {
         double z=m_beta[0];
         for(int f=0;f<FEATURE_COUNT;f++)
            z+=m_beta[f+1]*x_rows[r][f];
         double p=SafeSigmoid(z);
         double err=p-y_rows[r];
         grad[0]+=err;
         for(int f=0;f<FEATURE_COUNT;f++)
            grad[f+1]+=err*x_rows[r][f];
        }
      //--- level 2: average, add L2 on weights, take the gradient step
      grad[0]=grad[0]/rows;
      m_beta[0]=m_beta[0]-lr*grad[0];
      for(int f=0;f<FEATURE_COUNT;f++)
        {
         grad[f+1]=grad[f+1]/rows+l2*m_beta[f+1];
         m_beta[f+1]=m_beta[f+1]-lr*grad[f+1];
        }
     }
   m_train_rows_seen=rows;
   m_ready=true;
   return(true);
  }

//+------------------------------------------------------------------+
//| COnlineLogReg: Predict                                           |
//+------------------------------------------------------------------+
double COnlineLogReg::Predict(double &x_row[]) const
  {
   double z=m_beta[0];
   for(int f=0;f<FEATURE_COUNT;f++)
      z+=m_beta[f+1]*x_row[f];
   return(SafeSigmoid(z));
  }

#endif // ACTIVECONFIRM_LIB_MQH

Fit() refuses to run when the window has fewer than m_min_train_rows rows, so a cold EA waits instead of trading on a barely-trained model. Predict() uses the same SafeSigmoid as training.


Features and the Rolling Refit

All four features are price-derived: two log returns at different horizons, a centered RSI and an ATR-normalized distance from a fast SMA, clipped to plus or minus 3 ATR units and scaled. The label is plain: did price close higher InpLabelHorizonBars bars after the feature bar. The classifier's output is therefore meant to read as the probability that price closes higher, and the held-out results below are the test of whether it deserves that reading.

BuildFeatureRow (EA)

//+------------------------------------------------------------------+
//| BuildFeatureRow                                                  |
//+------------------------------------------------------------------+
bool BuildFeatureRow(const int shift,double &out_row[])
  {
   double close_arr[];
   ArraySetAsSeries(close_arr,true);
   if(CopyClose(_Symbol,_Period,0,shift+6,close_arr)<shift+6)
      return(false);
   double atr_arr[],rsi_arr[],sma_arr[];
   ArraySetAsSeries(atr_arr,true);
   ArraySetAsSeries(rsi_arr,true);
   ArraySetAsSeries(sma_arr,true);
   if(CopyBuffer(g_handle_atr,0,shift,1,atr_arr)<1)
      return(false);
   if(CopyBuffer(g_handle_rsi,0,shift,1,rsi_arr)<1)
      return(false);
   if(CopyBuffer(g_handle_sma,0,shift,1,sma_arr)<1)
      return(false);
   double atr=atr_arr[0];
   if(atr<=0.0)
      return(false);
   out_row[0]=MathLog(close_arr[shift]/close_arr[shift+1]);          // ret1
   out_row[1]=MathLog(close_arr[shift]/close_arr[shift+5]);          // ret5
   out_row[2]=(rsi_arr[0]/100.0)-0.5;                                // rsi_c
   double mom=(close_arr[shift]-sma_arr[0])/atr;
   if(mom>3.0)
      mom=3.0;
   if(mom<-3.0)
      mom=-3.0;
   out_row[3]=mom/3.0;                                               // atr_mom
   return(true);
  }

RefitModel (EA)

//+------------------------------------------------------------------+
//| RefitModel                                                       |
//+------------------------------------------------------------------+
void RefitModel(void)
  {
   int window=InpTrainWindowBars;
   int bars_needed=1+InpLabelHorizonBars+window+10;
   if(Bars(_Symbol,_Period)<bars_needed)
      return;
   double close_arr[];
   ArraySetAsSeries(close_arr,true);
   if(CopyClose(_Symbol,_Period,0,bars_needed,close_arr)<bars_needed)
      return;
   double x_rows[][FEATURE_COUNT];
   double y_rows[];
   ArrayResize(x_rows,window);
   ArrayResize(y_rows,window);
   int rows=0;
   //--- level 1: walk-forward window - feature bar i, label read off bar i-horizon
   for(int j=0;j<window;j++)
     {
      int i=1+InpLabelHorizonBars+j;
      double feat[FEATURE_COUNT];
      if(!BuildFeatureRow(i,feat))
         continue;
      int label_bar=i-InpLabelHorizonBars;
      double c_now=close_arr[i];
      double c_label=close_arr[label_bar];
      for(int f=0;f<FEATURE_COUNT;f++)
         x_rows[rows][f]=feat[f];
      y_rows[rows]=(c_label>c_now)?1.0:0.0;
      rows++;
     }
   if(rows<InpMinTrainRows)
      return;
   ArrayResize(x_rows,rows);
   ArrayResize(y_rows,rows);
   g_model.Fit(x_rows,y_rows,rows,InpGDIterations,InpLearningRate,InpL2Reg);
  }

The leakage guard is in the index arithmetic. For a training row built at offset i the label reads close_arr[i - InpLabelHorizonBars], a bar that has already closed by the time the refit runs. Mapping each j to i = 1 + InpLabelHorizonBars + j keeps every label bar inside the data available now.


The OnTick Decision Loop

OnTick() latches once per bar, resolves any open query, refits on schedule, scores the bar and routes the result. The latch uses iTime(_Symbol, _Period, 0), the bar that has just opened. The scored bar is shift 1, and scored_bar_time is what the audit log, the pending query and the calibration record all store. Orders go out on the first tick of the bar after the scored one.

Only one query can be open at a time, and while one is open the whole bar is skipped. In the tester the proxies answer on the same bar, so that never blocks anything there.

OnTick

//+------------------------------------------------------------------+
//| OnTick                                                           |
//+------------------------------------------------------------------+
void OnTick(void)
  {
   datetime bar_time=iTime(_Symbol,_Period,0);
   if(bar_time==g_last_bar_time)
      return;                    // act once per new bar, on the bar that just closed
   g_last_bar_time=bar_time;
   g_bar_counter++;
   //--- level 1: resolve or expire whatever review is already open first
   CheckPendingQuery();
   if(g_pending.active)
      return;                    // one open review at a time, by design
   //--- level 1: keep the classifier current
   if(!g_model.IsReady() || (g_bar_counter % InpRefitEveryBars)==0)
      RefitModel();
   if(!g_model.IsReady())
      return;
   //--- level 1: score the latest completed bar
   double feat[FEATURE_COUNT];
   if(!BuildFeatureRow(1,feat))
      return;
   datetime scored_bar_time=iTime(_Symbol,_Period,1);
   double p=g_model.Predict(feat);
   int direction=(p>=0.5)?1:-1;
   double u=Entropy(p);
   //--- level 1: every scored bar is recorded for the reliability check, whatever
   //--- zone it lands in; the grading happens after the run, in OnTester
   RecordCalibrationSample(scored_bar_time,p);
   //--- level 1: baseline mode bypasses all zone routing - every scored signal is
   //--- traded on sign(p-0.5) alone, the "no routing at all" control
   if(InpBaselineTradeAll)
     {
      bool base_ok=ExecuteSignal(direction,"BASELINE",g_magic_baseline);
      LogAuditRow(scored_bar_time,ZONE_BASELINE_ALL,p,u,-1.0,direction,base_ok,"BASELINE");
      return;
     }
   ENUM_QUERY_ZONE zone=ClassifyZone(u);
   double q_thresh=g_qtrack.IsReady()?g_qtrack.GetQuantile():-1.0;
   //--- level 1: route the signal
   if(zone==ZONE_AUTO_SKIP)
     {
      LogAuditRow(scored_bar_time,zone,p,u,q_thresh,direction,false,"-");
      return;
     }
   if(zone==ZONE_AUTO_TRADE)
     {
      bool ok=ExecuteSignal(direction,"AT",InpMagicNumber);
      LogAuditRow(scored_bar_time,zone,p,u,q_thresh,direction,ok,"AT");
      return;
     }
   //--- level 1: ZONE_QUERY - open a pending review and resolve it per proxy mode
   g_pending.active=true;
   g_pending.signal_time=scored_bar_time;
   g_pending.direction=direction;
   g_pending.probability=p;
   g_pending.uncertainty=u;
   g_pending.entry_price=(direction>0)?SymbolInfoDouble(_Symbol,SYMBOL_ASK):SymbolInfoDouble(_Symbol,SYMBOL_BID);
   g_pending.deadline=g_bar_counter+InpQueryTimeoutBars;
   if(InpHumanProxyMode==PROXY_LIVE)
     {
      OpenReviewButtons();
      if(InpEnablePushAlerts)
         SendNotification(StringFormat("ActiveConfirm: review requested, p=%.3f u=%.3f",p,u));
      Alert(StringFormat("ActiveConfirm: confirm or reject the %s signal on %s",
            (direction>0)?"BUY":"SELL",_Symbol));
      return;                    // wait for a click or a timeout on a later bar
     }
   //--- level 1: a proxy mode resolves the review immediately, same bar
   SDecisionResult res=ResolveProxyDecision(InpHumanProxyMode);
   bool taken=false;
   if(res.take_trade)
      taken=ExecuteSignal(g_pending.direction,res.tag,g_magic_qz);
   LogAuditRow(scored_bar_time,zone,p,u,q_thresh,direction,taken,res.tag);
   g_pending.active=false;
  }

ClassifyZone

//+------------------------------------------------------------------+
//| ClassifyZone                                                     |
//+------------------------------------------------------------------+
ENUM_QUERY_ZONE ClassifyZone(const double uncertainty)
  {
   //--- level 1: pure noise never touches the review budget
   if(uncertainty>InpAutoSkipCeiling)
      return(ZONE_AUTO_SKIP);
   g_qtrack.Update(uncertainty);
   //--- level 1: not enough history yet to trust the budget line - trade through
   if(!g_qtrack.IsReady())
      return(ZONE_AUTO_TRADE);
   if(uncertainty>=g_qtrack.GetQuantile())
      return(ZONE_QUERY);
   return(ZONE_AUTO_TRADE);
  }

Three behaviors in ClassifyZone matter for reading the results. First, g_qtrack.Update(u) runs before u is compared with the quantile, so a reading nudges the threshold it is about to be judged against. That is a coupling, not look-ahead, but it matters on legs with few signals. Second, until the tracker has seen five non-skipped readings, everything is auto-traded. Third, with InpBaselineTradeAll set, OnTick trades every scored signal on sign(p - 0.5) and returns before ClassifyZone, so the baseline cannot touch the quantile.

SDecisionResult ResolveProxyDecision(const ENUM_HUMAN_PROXY mode)
  {
   SDecisionResult res;
   res.take_trade=false;
   res.tag="QZ:REJECT";
   if(mode==PROXY_RANDOM)
     {
      //--- level 2: seeded once in OnInit when InpRandomSeed is non-zero;
      //--- with the default 0 no explicit seed is set
      if((MathRand()/32767.0)<0.5)
        {
         res.take_trade=true;
         res.tag="QZ:RANDOM";
        }
      else
         res.tag="QZ:RANDOM_REJECT";
     }
   else if(mode==PROXY_ALWAYS_CONFIRM)
     {
      res.take_trade=true;
      res.tag="QZ:ALWAYS_CONFIRM";
     }
   else if(mode==PROXY_ALWAYS_REJECT)
     {
      res.take_trade=false;
      res.tag="QZ:ALWAYS_REJECT";
     }
   return(res);
  }

//+------------------------------------------------------------------+
//| CheckPendingQuery                                                |
//+------------------------------------------------------------------+
void CheckPendingQuery(void)
  {
   if(!g_pending.active)
      return;
   if(g_bar_counter<g_pending.deadline)
      return;
   //--- level 1: fail-safe default - an unanswered query times out to no trade
   LogAuditRow(g_pending.signal_time,ZONE_QUERY,g_pending.probability,g_pending.uncertainty,
               g_qtrack.IsReady()?g_qtrack.GetQuantile():-1.0,
               g_pending.direction,false,"QZ:TIMEOUT");
   RemoveReviewButtons();
   g_pending.active=false;
  }

PROXY_RANDOM draws with MathRand(). OnInit calls MathSrand(InpRandomSeed) only when the seed is non-zero, so every seeded run replays exactly. I checked that: two reruns of EURUSD H1 with seed 1 reproduced the seed-1 pass of the series to the cent (net profit -133.93, 515 trades). With seed 0 no MathSrand call is made, and I did not test whether that is reproducible, which is why the series below uses explicit seeds only.

Every trade carries a tag in its order comment: AT for auto-trade, QZ:RANDOM, QZ:ALWAYS_CONFIRM and so on for the query zone, BASELINE for the control. The tags are how the results are split afterward. What each proxy tests is different. Always-confirm shows what the entropy gate does when its query zone is accepted whole. Always-reject shows what it does with the query zone removed. A coin flip adds randomness and no information.


Trade Execution and Risk Management

Execution applies four rules I hold every order-sending EA to:

  • size by margin, never trust the risk formula alone;
  • skip the trade if the normalized lot falls under the broker's minimum, never round up into it;
  • clamp stop and take-profit distance against the real stops and freeze levels plus spread and a pad, since some symbols report those as zero;
  • clamp the lot to both the volume limit and the volume max.

ExecuteSignal also detects which filling mode the symbol supports (fill-or-kill, then immediate-or-cancel, then return) instead of hard-coding one,. The lot is sized from equity, so profit in the tester scales with the account, a fact that shapes the comparisons later.

ExecuteSignal

//+------------------------------------------------------------------+
//| ExecuteSignal                                                    |
//+------------------------------------------------------------------+
bool ExecuteSignal(const int direction,const string tag,const int magic)
  {
   double sl=0.0,tp=0.0;
   if(!CalcStops(direction,sl,tp))
      return(false);
   double price=(direction>0)?SymbolInfoDouble(_Symbol,SYMBOL_ASK):SymbolInfoDouble(_Symbol,SYMBOL_BID);
   double sl_distance=MathAbs(price-sl);
   double lot=CalcSafeLot(sl_distance,direction,price);
   if(lot<=0.0)
      return(false);
   MqlTradeRequest request;
   MqlTradeResult  result;
   ZeroMemory(request);
   ZeroMemory(result);
   request.action=TRADE_ACTION_DEAL;
   request.symbol=_Symbol;
   request.volume=lot;
   request.type=(direction>0)?ORDER_TYPE_BUY:ORDER_TYPE_SELL;
   request.price=price;
   request.sl=sl;
   request.tp=tp;
   request.deviation=10;
   request.magic=magic;
   request.comment=tag;
   //--- level 1: use a filling mode the symbol actually supports
   long fill_flags=SymbolInfoInteger(_Symbol,SYMBOL_FILLING_MODE);
   if((fill_flags & SYMBOL_FILLING_FOK)!=0)
      request.type_filling=ORDER_FILLING_FOK;
   else if((fill_flags & SYMBOL_FILLING_IOC)!=0)
      request.type_filling=ORDER_FILLING_IOC;
   else
      request.type_filling=ORDER_FILLING_RETURN;
   if(!OrderSend(request,result))
     {
      Print("ActiveConfirm: OrderSend failed, error ",GetLastError());
      return(false);
     }
   if(result.retcode!=TRADE_RETCODE_DONE && result.retcode!=TRADE_RETCODE_PLACED)
     {
      Print("ActiveConfirm: OrderSend retcode ",result.retcode," - ",result.comment);
      return(false);
     }
   return(true);
  }

CalcSafeLot

//+------------------------------------------------------------------+
//| CalcSafeLot                                                      |
//+------------------------------------------------------------------+
double CalcSafeLot(const double sl_distance,const int direction,const double price)
  {
   if(sl_distance<=0.0)
      return(0.0);
   double tick_value=SymbolInfoDouble(_Symbol,SYMBOL_TRADE_TICK_VALUE);
   double tick_size=SymbolInfoDouble(_Symbol,SYMBOL_TRADE_TICK_SIZE);
   if(tick_size<=0.0)
      return(0.0);
   double risk_money=AccountInfoDouble(ACCOUNT_EQUITY)*InpRiskPercent/100.0;
   double loss_per_lot=(sl_distance/tick_size)*tick_value;
   if(loss_per_lot<=0.0)
      return(0.0);
   double lot=risk_money/loss_per_lot;
   //--- level 1: normalize to the symbol's volume step
   double vol_step=SymbolInfoDouble(_Symbol,SYMBOL_VOLUME_STEP);
   double vol_min=SymbolInfoDouble(_Symbol,SYMBOL_VOLUME_MIN);
   double vol_max=SymbolInfoDouble(_Symbol,SYMBOL_VOLUME_MAX);
   double vol_limit=SymbolInfoDouble(_Symbol,SYMBOL_VOLUME_LIMIT);
   if(vol_step>0.0)
      lot=MathFloor(lot/vol_step)*vol_step;
   //--- level 1: rule (2) - below the broker minimum means skip, never round up
   if(lot<vol_min)
      return(0.0);
   //--- level 1: rule (4) - clamp to both max and limit, not max alone
   if(vol_max>0.0 && lot>vol_max)
      lot=vol_max;
   if(vol_limit>0.0 && lot>vol_limit)
      lot=vol_limit;
   if(vol_step>0.0)
      lot=MathFloor(lot/vol_step)*vol_step;
   if(lot<vol_min)
      return(0.0);
   //--- level 1: rule (1) - clamp by actual margin, never trust the risk formula alone
   double margin_required=0.0;
   ENUM_ORDER_TYPE ot=(direction>0)?ORDER_TYPE_BUY:ORDER_TYPE_SELL;
   if(OrderCalcMargin(ot,_Symbol,lot,price,margin_required))
     {
      double free_margin=AccountInfoDouble(ACCOUNT_MARGIN_FREE);
      if(margin_required>free_margin && margin_required>0.0)
        {
         double scale=free_margin/margin_required;
         lot=lot*scale;
         if(vol_step>0.0)
            lot=MathFloor(lot/vol_step)*vol_step;
         if(lot<vol_min)
            return(0.0);
        }
     }
   return(lot);
  }

CalcStops

//+------------------------------------------------------------------+
//| CalcStops                                                        |
//+------------------------------------------------------------------+
bool CalcStops(const int direction,double &sl,double &tp)
  {
   double atr_arr[];
   ArraySetAsSeries(atr_arr,true);
   if(CopyBuffer(g_handle_atr,0,0,1,atr_arr)<1)
      return(false);
   double atr=atr_arr[0];
   if(atr<=0.0)
      return(false);
   double point=SymbolInfoDouble(_Symbol,SYMBOL_POINT);
   double spread_points=(double)SymbolInfoInteger(_Symbol,SYMBOL_SPREAD);
   long stops_level=SymbolInfoInteger(_Symbol,SYMBOL_TRADE_STOPS_LEVEL);
   long freeze_level=SymbolInfoInteger(_Symbol,SYMBOL_TRADE_FREEZE_LEVEL);
   //--- level 1: rule (3) - never trust stops/freeze level alone, some symbols report 0
   double min_points=(double)MathMax(stops_level,freeze_level);
   double safety_pad_points=5.0;
   double min_distance=(min_points+spread_points+safety_pad_points)*point;
   double sl_distance=MathMax(InpAtrMultSL*atr,min_distance);
   double tp_distance=MathMax(InpAtrMultTP*atr,min_distance);
   double ask=SymbolInfoDouble(_Symbol,SYMBOL_ASK);
   double bid=SymbolInfoDouble(_Symbol,SYMBOL_BID);
   if(direction>0)
     {
      sl=ask-sl_distance;
      tp=ask+tp_distance;
     }
   else
     {
      sl=bid+sl_distance;
      tp=bid-tp_distance;
     }
   return(true);
  }


Measuring the Split: OnTester, the Audit Log and the Attribution

When InpWriteAuditCsv is true, every routed decision gets one row in a per-bar CSV under MQL5\Files\ActiveConfirm\. The input was false in every published run, so no result in this article depends on the CSV. The tester account in all runs was a hedging account, which matters: every signal opens its own position, and every closing deal belongs to exactly one entry order. On a netting account positions merge into one and the per-tag attribution below would not be possible.

OnTester() sums closed-deal profit by magic number and prints it to the Journal with the oracle and floor lines. Those two lines are not bounds on the final result. The oracle adds the winning query trades that the current proxy actually took, so under reject-all it collapses to the auto-trade leg, and always-confirm is not a ceiling either, since the query zone can contain losers.

OnTester

//+------------------------------------------------------------------+
//| OnTester                                                         |
//+------------------------------------------------------------------+
double OnTester(void)
  {
   //--- level 1: grade every scored bar against what actually happened before
   //--- touching the PnL split - this is a read-only pass over already-closed
   //--- history, not a new decision, so it introduces no look-ahead of its own
   ComputeCalibration();
   double auto_pnl=0.0;
   double qz_pnl=0.0;
   double qz_positive_pnl=0.0;
   double baseline_pnl=0.0;
   int    qz_wins=0;
   int    qz_losses=0;
   int    baseline_trades=0;
   HistorySelect(0,TimeCurrent());
   int total=HistoryDealsTotal();
   //--- level 1: walk every closing deal this EA produced and split it by magic
   for(int i=0;i<total;i++)
     {
      ulong ticket=HistoryDealGetTicket(i);
      if(ticket==0)
         continue;
      long magic=HistoryDealGetInteger(ticket,DEAL_MAGIC);
      long entry=HistoryDealGetInteger(ticket,DEAL_ENTRY);
      if(entry!=DEAL_ENTRY_OUT)
         continue;
      double profit=HistoryDealGetDouble(ticket,DEAL_PROFIT)
                    +HistoryDealGetDouble(ticket,DEAL_SWAP)
                    +HistoryDealGetDouble(ticket,DEAL_COMMISSION);
      if(magic==InpMagicNumber)
         auto_pnl+=profit;
      else if(magic==g_magic_qz)
        {
         qz_pnl+=profit;
         if(profit>0.0)
           {
            qz_wins++;
            qz_positive_pnl+=profit;
           }
         else
            qz_losses++;
        }
      else if(magic==g_magic_baseline)
        {
         baseline_pnl+=profit;
         baseline_trades++;
        }
     }
   double oracle_pnl=auto_pnl+qz_positive_pnl;   // winners among the query trades taken
   double floor_pnl=auto_pnl;                    // auto-trade leg alone
   PrintFormat("ActiveConfirm OnTester | proxy=%d auto_pnl=%.2f qz_pnl=%.2f "
               "qz_wins=%d qz_losses=%d oracle_pnl=%.2f floor_pnl=%.2f "
               "baseline_pnl=%.2f baseline_trades=%d",
               InpHumanProxyMode,auto_pnl,qz_pnl,qz_wins,qz_losses,oracle_pnl,floor_pnl,
               baseline_pnl,baseline_trades);
   double pf=TesterStatistics(STAT_PROFIT_FACTOR);
   if(pf<=0.0 || !MathIsValidNumber(pf))
      return(auto_pnl+qz_pnl+baseline_pnl);
   return(pf);
  }

The split in the results section is computed independently from each saved report with report_split.py, shipped in MQL5\Files\ActiveConfirm\Python. The pairing is checked three ways: no closing deal is left unmatched, no entry is left open, and the per-tag profits add up to the report's net profit with a residual of 0.00 on all 32 reports.

The matching has one soft spot. Across the 16 held-out reports there were 298 closings with more than one candidate entry, and in only 3 of them did the candidates carry different tags. At most those 3 trades could have changed bucket, which is too few to move any conclusion below.

ComputeCalibration

//+------------------------------------------------------------------+
//| ComputeCalibration                                               |
//+------------------------------------------------------------------+
void ComputeCalibration(void)
  {
   if(g_calib_count<1)
      return;
   //--- level 1: five equal-width probability bins, plus a running Brier-score sum
   double bin_sum_p[5],bin_sum_actual[5];
   int    bin_n[5];
   ArrayInitialize(bin_sum_p,0.0);
   ArrayInitialize(bin_sum_actual,0.0);
   ArrayInitialize(bin_n,0);
   double brier_sum=0.0;
   int    graded=0;
   int    file=INVALID_HANDLE;
   if(InpWriteAuditCsv)
     {
      string path=StringFormat("ActiveConfirm\\calibration_%d.csv",InpMagicNumber);
      file=FileOpen(path,FILE_WRITE|FILE_CSV|FILE_ANSI,',');
      if(file!=INVALID_HANDLE)
         FileWrite(file,"scored_bar_time","predicted_p","actual_up");
     }
   //--- level 1: grade every recorded sample whose label horizon has already closed -
   //--- this reuses the exact same "close InpLabelHorizonBars later" definition
   //--- RefitModel trains against, so the grade means what the model was actually fit to
   for(int i=0;i<g_calib_count;i++)
     {
      int shift_scored=iBarShift(_Symbol,_Period,g_calib_time[i],true);
      if(shift_scored<0)
         continue;                 // exact bar not found - skip rather than grade the wrong one
      int shift_outcome=shift_scored-InpLabelHorizonBars;
      if(shift_outcome<0)
         continue;                 // horizon runs past the end of available history
      double close_scored=iClose(_Symbol,_Period,shift_scored);
      double close_outcome=iClose(_Symbol,_Period,shift_outcome);
      if(close_scored<=0.0 || close_outcome<=0.0)
         continue;
      double actual=(close_outcome>close_scored)?1.0:0.0;
      double p=g_calib_prob[i];
      brier_sum+=(p-actual)*(p-actual);
      graded++;
      int bin=(int)MathFloor(p*5.0);
      if(bin>4)
         bin=4;
      if(bin<0)
         bin=0;
      bin_sum_p[bin]+=p;
      bin_sum_actual[bin]+=actual;
      bin_n[bin]++;
      if(file!=INVALID_HANDLE)
         FileWrite(file,TimeToString(g_calib_time[i],TIME_DATE|TIME_MINUTES),
                    DoubleToString(p,5),(int)actual);
     }
   if(file!=INVALID_HANDLE)
      FileClose(file);
   if(graded<1)
     {
      Print("ActiveConfirm calibration: no graded samples - horizon never closed within this run");
      return;
     }
   //--- level 1: report the reliability table and the Brier score to the Journal -
   //--- a well-calibrated model has mean_predicted close to actual_up_rate in every bin
   PrintFormat("ActiveConfirm calibration | graded_samples=%d brier_score=%.4f",
               graded,brier_sum/graded);
   for(int b=0;b<5;b++)
     {
      if(bin_n[b]<1)
        {
         PrintFormat("  bin [%.1f,%.1f): n=0",b*0.2,(b+1)*0.2);
         continue;
        }
      PrintFormat("  bin [%.1f,%.1f): n=%d mean_predicted=%.3f actual_up_rate=%.3f",
                  b*0.2,(b+1)*0.2,bin_n[b],bin_sum_p[b]/bin_n[b],bin_sum_actual[b]/bin_n[b]);
     }
  }

The ProbView Indicator

The audit CSV, when enabled, is a table rather than a picture. ActiveConfirm_ProbView plots P(up), uncertainty, the dynamic query threshold and the fixed ceiling in a sub-window, recomputed in OnCalculate so it works on any chart. A full recalculation replays the walk-forward pass statefully from the first bar with enough history.

The indicator is a visual aid and does not replay exactly what the EA traded. The EA counts bars from the start of its run and refits when that counter is a multiple of InpRefitEveryBars. The indicator refits on the absolute bar index of the chart, starts from the first bar with enough history, and reprocesses the last bar on every tick, so its quantile tracker can see one bar's reading more than once. On an arbitrary chart the plotted threshold can therefore differ from the one the EA traded against. The two agree only with an identical replay window and refit schedule. I did not synchronize them, and no result here comes from the indicator.

//+------------------------------------------------------------------+
//| OnCalculate                                                      |
//+------------------------------------------------------------------+
int OnCalculate(const int rates_total,const int prev_calculated,const datetime &time[],
                 const double &open[],const double &high[],const double &low[],
                 const double &close[],const long &tick_volume[],const long &volume[],
                 const int &spread[])
  {
   int warmup=InpLabelHorizonBars+6;
   if(rates_total<warmup+InpMinTrainRows)
      return(0);
   //--- level 1: a full recalculation must replay as one honest, stateful pass
   if(prev_calculated==0)
     {
      g_qtrack.Init(1.0-InpReviewBudgetPct/100.0);
      g_model.Init(InpMinTrainRows);
     }
   double atr_full[],rsi_full[],sma_full[];
   if(CopyBuffer(g_handle_atr,0,0,rates_total,atr_full)<rates_total)
      return(prev_calculated);
   if(CopyBuffer(g_handle_rsi,0,0,rates_total,rsi_full)<rates_total)
      return(prev_calculated);
   if(CopyBuffer(g_handle_sma,0,0,rates_total,sma_full)<rates_total)
      return(prev_calculated);
   int start=(prev_calculated>1)?prev_calculated-1:warmup;
   //--- level 1: walk forward bar by bar - never touch a bar past "now"
   for(int i=start;i<rates_total;i++)
     {
      if(i<warmup)
         continue;
      if((i % InpRefitEveryBars)==0 || !g_model.IsReady())
         RefitModel(i,close,atr_full,rsi_full,sma_full);
      if(!g_model.IsReady())
        {
         BufferProb[i]=EMPTY_VALUE;
         BufferUncertainty[i]=EMPTY_VALUE;
         BufferThreshold[i]=EMPTY_VALUE;
         BufferCeiling[i]=InpAutoSkipCeiling;
         continue;
        }
      double feat[FEATURE_COUNT];
      if(!BuildFeatureRow(i,close,atr_full,rsi_full,sma_full,feat))
        {
         BufferProb[i]=EMPTY_VALUE;
         BufferUncertainty[i]=EMPTY_VALUE;
         BufferThreshold[i]=EMPTY_VALUE;
         BufferCeiling[i]=InpAutoSkipCeiling;
         continue;
        }
      double p=g_model.Predict(feat);
      double u=Entropy(p);
      BufferProb[i]=p;
      BufferUncertainty[i]=u;
      BufferCeiling[i]=InpAutoSkipCeiling;
      if(u<=InpAutoSkipCeiling)
         g_qtrack.Update(u);
      BufferThreshold[i]=g_qtrack.IsReady()?g_qtrack.GetQuantile():EMPTY_VALUE;
     }
   return(rates_total);
  }

BuildFeatureRow and RefitModel (indicator)

//+------------------------------------------------------------------+
//| BuildFeatureRow                                                  |
//+------------------------------------------------------------------+
bool BuildFeatureRow(const int i,const double &close[],const double &atr_full[],
                      const double &rsi_full[],const double &sma_full[],double &out_row[])
  {
   if(i<5)
      return(false);
   double atr=atr_full[i];
   if(atr<=0.0)
      return(false);
   out_row[0]=MathLog(close[i]/close[i-1]);                 // ret1
   out_row[1]=MathLog(close[i]/close[i-5]);                 // ret5
   out_row[2]=(rsi_full[i]/100.0)-0.5;                       // rsi_c
   double mom=(close[i]-sma_full[i])/atr;
   if(mom>3.0)
      mom=3.0;
   if(mom<-3.0)
      mom=-3.0;
   out_row[3]=mom/3.0;                                       // atr_mom
   return(true);
  }

//+------------------------------------------------------------------+
//| RefitModel                                                       |
//+------------------------------------------------------------------+
void RefitModel(const int upto_i,const double &close[],const double &atr_full[],
                 const double &rsi_full[],const double &sma_full[])
  {
   int window=InpTrainWindowBars;
   int last_feat=upto_i-InpLabelHorizonBars;
   int start=last_feat-window+1;
   if(start<5)
      return;
   double x_rows[][FEATURE_COUNT];
   double y_rows[];
   ArrayResize(x_rows,window);
   ArrayResize(y_rows,window);
   int rows=0;
   //--- level 1: every training row's label bar is <= upto_i - already known
   for(int k=start;k<=last_feat;k++)
     {
      double feat[FEATURE_COUNT];
      if(!BuildFeatureRow(k,close,atr_full,rsi_full,sma_full,feat))
         continue;
      int label_bar=k+InpLabelHorizonBars;
      for(int f=0;f<FEATURE_COUNT;f++)
         x_rows[rows][f]=feat[f];
      y_rows[rows]=(close[label_bar]>close[k])?1.0:0.0;
      rows++;
     }
   if(rows<InpMinTrainRows)
      return;
   ArrayResize(x_rows,rows);
   ArrayResize(y_rows,rows);
   g_model.Fit(x_rows,y_rows,rows,InpGDIterations,InpLearningRate,InpL2Reg);
  }

Two measurement choices. First, position size is a fraction of equity, so a run that loses early trades smaller later and a run that wins trades larger. Net profit therefore mixes trade quality with sizing. For comparing runs I use profit per lot per trade, meaning (profit + swap + commission) divided by the trade's volume, which removes the sizing. Second, the confidence intervals are 90 percent bootstrap intervals over trades (5,000 resamples, fixed seeds). Trades overlap in time, so these intervals are narrower than they should be, and I treat an interval that merely excludes zero as weak evidence.

The two M15 cells look attractive, but they have 26 to 103 trades each and come from a setting that lost in the window it was tuned on. The H1 cells have 6 to 10 trades and say nothing. In short:

  • Routing beats trade-everything on net profit and drawdown on all four legs, though mostly by trading less.
  • Beyond trading less, entropy selection is supported on one leg out of four, GBPUSD M15, and that leg lost in tuning.
  • The query zone beats the auto zone on all four legs by a margin no interval confirms.
  • The ceiling that won tuning produced a profit under only one proxy on one leg.
  • The held-out Brier scores sit at 0.25, the level of a constant 0.5 forecast, so the classifier shows no measurable skill.

Limits that remain: commission is zero and the spread comes from the tick feed, so there is no cost test, which matters most on M15; the held-out window is eleven weeks on one feed; the H1 legs have 250 to 570 trades against roughly 1,200 to 1,500 on M15; I compared four legs and several statistics, so some intervals will look better than they are; the classifier and risk settings were not swept; and the interval method treats overlapping trades as independent.


Conclusion

The routing layer works as software: it scores, skips, queries and trades, every decision is tagged, and the profit split is exact on hedging accounts. As a trading idea it has not yet shown an edge. The checkpoint's value stays open, and a human proxy that is a coin flip cannot answer it.

The part worth copying is the protocol: choose the ceiling on one window by a written rule, judge it on another, compare against matched-frequency and seeded controls, and split profit exactly instead of by hand. The next steps are more symbols and periods, repeated held-out windows, a real cost model with commission and a stressed spread, a sensitivity check on the training window, review budget, learning rate, L2 and the stop and target, and a human-in-the-loop run on a demo account, where PROXY_LIVE is the mode to use.
